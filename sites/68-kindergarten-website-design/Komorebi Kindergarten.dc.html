ABOUT US Our Philosophy

Helping every child discover their own potential.

At Komorebi, we believe children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and free to follow their curiosity. Our days are built around play, nature, and close relationships, so each child can explore at their own pace.

Teachers observe closely and set goals for each class every term, working together with families so that every child grows with confidence, kindness, and a love of learning.

— Principal Keiko Hayashi —
