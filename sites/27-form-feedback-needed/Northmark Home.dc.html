Who we are

COMPANY INTRODUCTION

We are a team of forty strategists, media buyers, engineers and analysts who treat marketing as an operating system rather than a campaign.

Northmark was founded in 2015 by three practitioners who were tired of reporting that flattered the agency and starved the client. Every engagement starts with the same discipline: agree what a customer is worth, instrument the funnel so the number is trustworthy, then buy growth against it.

That approach has carried brands in B2B software, retail, healthcare and finance from first paid channel to category leadership. We publish our methods, price transparently, and take on a limited number of clients so senior people stay on the work.
