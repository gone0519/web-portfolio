Care Designed Around Your Body and Your Life.

At Kinari Clinic we start by listening. Every visit is one-on-one with a licensed osteopath who takes time to understand where it hurts, how it started, and what your days look like — your work, your sport, your sleep. From there we assess posture, joints and movement, and build a treatment plan that fits you, with clear explanations at every step and simple care you can continue at home.

TREATMENTS

What We Can Help With

01

Neck, Shoulder & Back Pain

Gentle manual therapy to ease muscle tension and restore movement in the neck, shoulders and upper back — often linked to desk work, phone use and stress.

Common concerns: stiff shoulders, headaches from tension, limited neck rotation
Suited to: office workers, drivers, parents of young children

Book this treatment ›
02

Lower Back & Pelvic Alignment

We look at the whole chain — hips, pelvis and spine — to understand why your lower back is under strain, then work to improve balance and mobility.

Common concerns: recurring lower back pain, hip discomfort, postpartum pelvic care
Suited to: anyone who sits or lifts for long periods

Book this treatment ›
03

Sports Injuries & Joint Pain

Care for sprains, strains and overuse injuries, with guidance on safe return to training. Our team has supported local school and club athletes for over 15 years.

Common concerns: knee and ankle pain, shoulder injuries, muscle strains
Suited to: students, runners, weekend and competitive athletes

Book this treatment ›
04

Rehabilitation & Accident Care

Step-by-step recovery support after injuries, including traffic accidents. We can help with insurance paperwork and coordinate with your doctor where needed.

Common concerns: whiplash, reduced strength after injury, stiffness after surgery
Suited to: patients recovering from accidents or long periods of rest

KENTA MORISHITA, D.O.
